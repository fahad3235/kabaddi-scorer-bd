    Scoring rules (simplified standard kabaddi)

    • Raid point: +1 — raider touches an opponent or crosses the bonus line safely.
    • Bonus point: +1 — raider crosses the bonus line with a foot in the air.
    • Tackle point: +1 — defenders stop the raider.
    • Super tackle: +2 — a successful tackle when 3 or fewer defenders are on court.
    • All-out: +2 — awarded to the team that puts all opponents out.

    This is a casual scorekeeper using simplified rules. It is NOT tournament-certified and carries no live match data.
